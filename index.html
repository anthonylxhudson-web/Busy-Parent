Questions

Things new parents always ask

I've never heard of you — how do I know this is legit?
+
We cook out of a licensed, Santa Clara County Health Department–inspected commercial kitchen in Willow Glen. Your first order comes with a 100% money-back guarantee — if you're not happy, you get every dollar back, no questions asked. We'd rather earn your trust than keep your money.
Do you serve pork?
+
Never. Our menu is beef, chicken, and turkey only — period. We made that call intentionally so more families can eat without worry.
How does delivery work and where do you deliver?
+
We deliver to any address in the greater San Jose area within a 45-minute drive of Willow Glen — that covers most of the city and surrounding areas. Delivery is available any day of the week for an additional $15. Not sure if you're in range? Text us and we'll confirm in minutes.
These meals are for adults — what about my kids?
+
That's exactly the point. If you have a newborn, they don't need brisket — you do. We portion for 2 adults. If you have toddlers or older kids, the meals stretch and kids generally love this food. But the seasoning, protein size, and portions are designed for grown-ups who need a real meal.
Do I have to subscribe or commit to anything?
+
Zero commitment. Order once, skip a week, come back whenever. No subscription, no cancellation hoops, nothing. We want to earn your repeat business every single week — not trap you into it.
How long does the food stay good?
+
Safe in the fridge for 5 days from when you receive it. Every container is labeled with a use-by date. You can also freeze any meal for up to 2 months — great for stocking up when you're in a really hard stretch.
